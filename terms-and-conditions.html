Effective Date: 1 January 2025 | Last Updated: March 2026

Legal Framework: These terms govern the use of Sellolla, a platform operating as a multi-vendor marketplace, affiliate network, and provider of national and international dropshipping services.

1. Intermediary Status and Liability

Sellolla operates as a digital intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. We maintain "Safe Harbor" protection by not initiating transmissions or modifying content provided by third-party sellers. However, under "fall-back liability" rules, Sellolla remains responsible if a seller fails to deliver goods or services as promised.

2. Dropshipping Operations

A. National (Domestic) Dropshipping

  • Sellolla facilitates the sale of goods from Indian suppliers to domestic consumers.
  • All domestic transactions are subject to GST 2.0 regulations, including applicable Tax Collected at Source (TCS).

B. International Dropshipping

  • International products are sourced from foreign suppliers and imported into India.
  • We utilize a Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) model, where all customs duties and IGST are included in the checkout price.
  • Imported electronics must carry BIS and WPC certifications, while food products must meet FSSAI standards.
  • Sellolla or its partners maintain a valid Importer Exporter Code (IEC) for all commercial imports.

3. Multi-Vendor Marketplace Governance

  • All sellers must undergo Aadhaar-based KYC and provide valid GST registration details for traceability.
  • Sellers are legally responsible for the accuracy of product images, descriptions, and quality claims.
  • The contract of sale is strictly between the buyer and the third-party seller.

4. Affiliate Marketing Program

  • Affiliates are legally mandated to disclose their promotional relationship with Sellolla as per ASCI guidelines.
  • Prohibited practices include Cookie Stuffing, Brand Bidding on trademarked keywords, and making misleading product claims.
  • Fraudulent activity results in immediate termination and forfeiture of commissions.

5. Payments and Financial Safety

  • We implement 3D Secure 2.0 and multi-factor authentication to ensure transaction security.
  • To prevent chargebacks, we maintain detailed Proof of Delivery and provide real-time order tracking.
  • Bank statements will reflect "SELLOLLA.IN" for transparent billing.

6. Returns and Consumer Rights

  • Eligible items can be returned within 7 calendar days of delivery.
  • Refunds are initiated via the original payment method and typically reflect within 1–7 business days.
  • Digital products and perishable goods are generally non-returnable unless defective.

7. Data Privacy and Governing Law

  • As a "Data Fiduciary" under the DPDP Act 2023, we process personal data only for consented purposes.
  • Transaction records are retained for 7 years as mandated by Indian tax laws.
  • Disputes are subject to Indian law, with consumers having the right to file claims in their local jurisdiction under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
