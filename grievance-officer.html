As mandated by the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 — Rule 4(4)(vi) and Rule 6(8)

Your consumer rights matter. We are legally required to appoint a Grievance Officer to address complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days. If we fall short, you have the right to escalate to national consumer authorities.

What Issues Can the Grievance Officer Handle?

  • Disputes about orders, refunds, or returns that were not resolved by our support team
  • Complaints about seller conduct or fraudulent listings
  • Privacy concerns or data protection requests
  • Issues with payment deductions without order fulfillment
  • Complaints about misleading product descriptions
  • Any consumer complaint under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019

How to File a Grievance

1
Try Customer Support First Email support@sellolla.in or use the contact form. Most issues are resolved here within 24–48 hours.
2
Email the Grievance Officer If not resolved, email grievance@sellolla.in with subject: "Consumer Grievance – [Your Order Number]"
3
Include These Details Your full name · Order number · Contact number · Date of purchase · Description of the issue · Supporting photos or screenshots
4
Receive Acknowledgement We will acknowledge your grievance within 48 hours with a complaint reference number.
5
Resolution Your complaint will be investigated and resolved within 30 days of receipt.

Further Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our resolution, you have the right to escalate to the following government authorities — completely free of charge:

📞
National Consumer Helpline

Call 1915 (Toll Free)
Available in multiple languages

E-Daakhil Portal

File online consumer complaints at edaakhil.nic.in

🏛️
Consumer Forum

File at your District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (DCDRC)

🌐
Consumer Affairs Ministry

Visit consumeraffairs.nic.in for guidance and resources
