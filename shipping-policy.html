Shipping Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2025  |  Last Updated: March 2025  |  Applies to all physical products on shop.sellolla.in

🚚 Free Shipping on all orders above ₹499 from participating sellers. Shipping charges (if any) are always shown clearly before payment — no hidden fees.

1. Delivery Timeframes

Delivery times depend on the seller's location, your delivery address, and the shipping method chosen.

Shipping Type Estimated Delivery Cost

Dropship / Sourced Products 7–15 business days As shown at checkout

Business days = Monday to Saturday, excluding national public holidays. Delivery to remote or hilly areas may take 2–3 additional days.

2. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are set individually by each seller. The exact shipping cost for your order will be displayed on the product page and in your cart — before you complete payment. We never add surprise shipping fees at the end.

Products with the "Free Shipping" badge have no delivery charge. For orders above ₹499, you may also see a "Free Standard Shipping" option at checkout from qualifying sellers.

3. Order Processing Time

Most sellers process and dispatch orders within 1–2 business days of receiving payment confirmation. Some handmade or custom products may take 3–5 business days to prepare before dispatch. This will be mentioned on the product page.

⏱️ Note: Estimated delivery = processing time + shipping time. For example, 1 day processing + 3 days shipping = delivery in approximately 4 business days.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive:

  • An email with your tracking number and courier name
  • An SMS notification to your registered mobile number

To track your order:

  1. Log in → My Orders → select your order → click "Track Order"
  2. Or enter your tracking number directly on the courier's website

5. Delivery Failure & Re-delivery

If delivery fails because of an incorrect address or the recipient is unavailable, the courier will make up to 2 delivery attempts. After 2 failed attempts, the package will be held at the nearest hub for 3–5 days before being returned to the seller.

If you need to update your delivery address after placing an order, contact us immediately at support@sellolla.in — address changes are only possible before the order is dispatched.

6. Damaged, Lost or Delayed Shipments

If your item arrives damaged:

  1. Take photos or a video before opening the package
  2. Note the damage on the delivery receipt when possible, or refuse delivery
  3. Contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos: support@sellolla.in
  4. We will arrange a replacement or full refund

If your order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window, contact us with your order number and we will investigate with the courier partner.

If your order shows "Delivered" but you didn't receive it: Check with neighbours and your building reception. If still not found, contact us within 48 hours and we will raise a dispute with the courier.

7. Shipping to Remote Areas

We deliver across India including Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. However, some remote PIN codes, hilly areas (parts of North-East, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep) may have limited courier availability or extended delivery times. This will be indicated at checkout if applicable.

8. Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available for eligible products. When you pay COD, please keep exact change ready. The courier collects payment at delivery. Note: COD orders have a higher cancellation rate — please only place COD orders if you genuinely intend to purchase.

💡 Tip: Paying online (UPI, card, net banking) gives you faster processing, guaranteed delivery, and easier returns compared to COD.
