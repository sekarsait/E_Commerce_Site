Return & Refund Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2025  |  Last Updated: March 2025  |  Applies to: shop.sellolla.in

📦 Quick Summary: You can return most items within 7 days of delivery. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

1. Return Window

You may return eligible items within 7 calendar days from the date of delivery. The return request must be raised before the 7-day window closes.

2. Eligible Reasons for Return

  • Item received is damaged or defective
  • Item is not as described on the product listing
  • You received the wrong item
  • Item is incomplete — missing parts or accessories listed in the product description

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following are not eligible for return unless they arrive damaged or defective:

  • Digital products and downloadable files
  • Perishable items (food, flowers, plants)
  • Customised or personalised products
  • Intimate or sanitary goods (for hygiene reasons)
  • Hazardous materials, flammable liquids or gases
  • Items explicitly marked "Non-Returnable" on the product page

Note: If a "Non-Returnable" product arrives damaged or defective, you are still entitled to a replacement or refund. Please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos.

4. How to Raise a Return Request

  1. Log in to your Sellolla account
  2. Go to My Orders → select the order → click "Request Return"
  3. Select the reason and upload clear photographs of the item
  4. Submit — you will receive a confirmation email within 24 hours
  5. Our team will review and approve or decline the request within 48 business hours

You can also email us directly at support@sellolla.in with your order number and photos.

5. Return Shipping

For approved returns due to our error (wrong item, damage, defect), we cover the return shipping cost. For returns due to change of mind (where applicable), the buyer bears the return shipping cost.

Please do not send items back without receiving a return approval — unapproved returns may not be processed.

6. Refund Timeline

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund will be processed as follows:

  • UPI: 1–3 business days
  • Debit / Credit Card: 5–7 business days (depending on your bank)
  • Net Banking: 3–5 business days
  • Cash on Delivery: Refunded to your bank account within 5–7 business days (we will collect your account details)
  • Wallet / Store Credit: Instantly on approval

💡 Tip: You will receive an email notification at each step — when your return is approved, when we receive the item, and when the refund is initiated.

7. Cancellation Policy

You can cancel an order before it is shipped. To cancel, go to My Orders → select the order → click "Cancel Order".

Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled. Please use the return process instead after receiving the item.

Full refund will be issued for cancelled pre-shipment orders within 3–5 business days.

8. Seller Responsibility

Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, all sellers on Sellolla are legally required to honour genuine return requests. If a seller refuses a valid return request, please escalate directly to our Grievance Officer (details below).

9. Disputes & Escalation

If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided:

  • Escalate to our Grievance Officer
  • File a complaint at the National Consumer Helpline — 1915 (Toll Free)
  • File online at E-Daakhil Portal
