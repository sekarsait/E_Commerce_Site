Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2025  |  Last Updated: March 2025  |  Applies to: shop.sellolla.in

Our Promise: We do not sell your personal data. Ever. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

This Privacy Policy describes how Sellolla ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information. It is prepared in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and applicable provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

1. Information We Collect

Account Information — When you register: name, email address, phone number, profile photo. Passwords are stored as encrypted hashes and are never readable by our team.

Transaction Information — Orders placed, products purchased, payment method used (we do not store full card numbers — all payment processing is handled by Razorpay and PayPal, both of which are PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant).

Usage Information — Pages visited, products viewed, search queries, time spent on pages, device type, browser type, IP address, and referring website.

Location — City and state level, only if you use location-based search features. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Communications — Messages sent between buyers and sellers through our on-platform messaging system.

Seller Information — If you sell on Sellolla: business name, GST number, bank account details for payouts, identity verification documents.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process orders and facilitate transactions
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, invoices
  • Verify seller identity and prevent fraud
  • Respond to customer service requests
  • Improve product recommendations and search results
  • Send promotional emails — you can unsubscribe at any time
  • Comply with legal obligations under Indian law
  • Resolve disputes between buyers and sellers

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to any third party. We share it only in these limited cases:

  • With Sellers: Your name, delivery address, and phone number are shared with the seller when you place an order
  • Payment Processors: Razorpay, PayPal — for secure payment processing only
  • Logistics Partners: Courier companies receive your delivery address to fulfil shipments
  • Legal Requirement: When required by Indian law, court order, or government authority under the IT Act or DPDPA
  • Business Transfer: In the event of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — with at least 30 days prior notice to users

4. Data Security

We implement the following security measures as required by the IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers
  • Bcrypt hashing for all stored passwords
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls — staff can only access data necessary for their role
  • Automatic session expiry and CSRF protection

Important: No system is 100% secure. If you suspect your account has been compromised, change your password immediately and contact us at support@sellolla.in.

5. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — Required to keep you logged in and remember your shopping cart. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics, to understand how users navigate our site. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.
  • Preference cookies — Remember your currency and language choice.

You can manage cookies in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies will prevent some features from working.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, we retain transaction records for 7 years as required by Indian tax and accounting laws (GST Act, Companies Act). All other data is deleted within 90 days of account closure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has registered on our platform, please contact us immediately and we will delete the account.

9. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites (payment processors, social media, courier tracking pages). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. For significant changes, we will notify you by email or a prominent notice on the website at least 15 days before the changes take effect. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes your acceptance.
